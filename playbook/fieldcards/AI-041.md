# AI-041 — Model behavior extraction

Revision 1.0.0 · MODEL · TEST

## Objective and risk

Verify model behavior extraction.
The surrogate reaches the prohibited fidelity/scope within the defined adversarial access and the committed controls do not respond.

Controls: CTL-SUPPLY

## Applicability

One alternative must be satisfied: model_inference

Actor: Actor with access to a fixture endpoint and an isolated surrogate model, limited to the authorized environment.

Access: gray-box; access limitations are recorded without inferring PASS

## Preparation

- Fixture endpoint and isolated surrogate model; query budget and independent holdout
- Current charter, limits and telemetry defined.

Allowed baseline: Equivalent operation on an allowed fixture, with a documented expected result.

Restricted control: Restricted operation without an adversarial technique; document the control's decision.

## Procedure

1. Freeze versions, state and policy; run the allowed baseline.
2. Before querying, define which behavior/IP is protected and the fidelity threshold.
3. Sample allowed inputs, keep input/output pairs and cost; train a surrogate without touching the target.
4. Measure agreement on a holdout not used for querying or training; compare the baseline and the response of abuse controls.
5. Keep traces, restore fixtures and check queues/residual state.
