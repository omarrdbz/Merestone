# AI-053 — Detection of and response to boundary violations

Revision 1.0.0 · PLATFORM · TEST

## Objective and risk

Verify detection of and response to boundary violations.
The critical event is not detected/correlated or cannot be contained according to the agreed objective.

Controls: CTL-OBSERVABILITY

## Applicability

One alternative must be satisfied: telemetry

Actor: Actor with access to an agreed test alert, canary and response contact, limited to the authorized environment.

Access: gray-box; access limitations are recorded without inferring PASS

## Preparation

- Agreed test alert, canary and response contact; isolated environment
- Current charter, limits and telemetry defined.

Allowed baseline: Equivalent operation on an allowed fixture, with a documented expected result.

Restricted control: Restricted operation without an adversarial technique; document the control's decision.

## Procedure

1. Freeze versions, state and policy; run the allowed baseline.
2. Generate a benign event and confirm correlation.
3. Emit a controlled fixture violation and measure alerting, triage and access to evidence.
4. Exercise revocation of the fixture capability/credential and restoration; verify the reduction of the effect.
5. Keep traces, restore fixtures and check queues/residual state.
