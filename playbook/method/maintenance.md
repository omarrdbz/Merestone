# Maintenance and contributions

## Canonical source

The editable catalogs are `catalog/{cases,controls,techniques,examples,scenarios,mappings}/index.json`, plus `catalog/capabilities.json` and the reference snapshot in `catalog/sources/`. The fieldcards, the catalog and technique indexes, the scenarios and the walkthroughs are generated with `python fieldguide.py render`. Do not hand-edit a generated view.

Text files use LF line endings, the same that `render` writes. `MANIFEST.sha256` records the bytes of every file; regenerate it after any change.

Retired IDs are not reused. AI-060 was reserved during the construction of 0.3 and retired to avoid duplicating AI-038; the catalog states this in its opening note.

## Adding content

1. Define objective, threat, capabilities, boundary and control/invariant. Check whether it already exists.
2. Choose the type: control, case, technique, example variant or scenario. A new text string for the same mechanism is usually a variant.
3. Write preconditions, baseline, procedure, reachability/effect, evidence, oracle, false positives, limits, cleanup and retest.
4. Add typed relations and justified mappings. A conceptual reference does not demonstrate that a procedure verifies a control.
5. Record the source and edition of any external reference in the mappings, and every adaptation of an example.
6. Validate the catalog and schema, regenerate views and check a lab usage example. Independent review is required before declaring external technical validation.

An ID is never reused; if a case is split, record the new IDs in its revision notes and justify it. New capabilities extend the vocabulary without changing the assessment cycle.

## Versioning

Framework, schema, catalog, case, technique, example and adapter have their own versions. A change to the oracle/precondition/evidence increases the semantic revision and invalidates plans if the snapshot changes. An editorial adjustment is also recorded, but must not delete earlier evidence.

For a new plan based on a previous one: keep the original, create a revised architecture, generate new obligations and record in `amendments` the previous plan, reason, approver and date. Do not recalculate the hashes of a historical plan to hide differences.

## Examples and walkthroughs

Example inputs and the per-family walkthroughs are Merestone-original and regenerated by `render` from the catalog. They are self-contained. When adding an example, keep it a fixture against a reserved canary and record its technique link.

## Change review

The verification suite must detect: orphan references, reused IDs, stale generated views, profiles that activate nonexistent components, unknown capabilities treated as absent, deleted tasks, evidence without a hash, hidden FAIL, retests that overwrite the original and incompatible plans compared silently.

Software validations test contracts. Local pilots test mechanisms and export. An external assessment and review by another engineer test usefulness on a real system; do not substitute one category for another when describing the state of the delivery.
